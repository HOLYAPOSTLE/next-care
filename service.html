General and Family Medicine

Our department has highly experienced specialists in family medicine (FM). These FM specialists provide the best medical response to your condition, ensuring comprehensive care for patients of all ages.

Our FM specialists work together with the general laboratory service to confirm a diagnosis before appropriate management is given. From routine check-ups to managing chronic conditions, our family medicine team is dedicated to keeping you and your family healthy.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about visiting Oak Specialist Hospital.

1. Today is my first visit to OAK. Where do I start?

Kindly speak to our front desk personnel and inform them of your status as a first timer. Your basic data will be taken into our system and your preferred mode of payment would be recorded, including cash or credit based on insurance/employer coverage.

2. I am a foreigner; do I need a special registration?

No, we are all one. Where applicable, submit all the necessary referral forms and insurance documents to aid in the registration processes.

3. Do you take health insurance?

Yes, we accept a wide range of private health insurance claims. Kindly click here for more info.

4. Do you accept card payments?

Yes, we accept credit/debit cards including VISA, GHLink, and MasterCard. We also accept foreign currencies at an approved exchange rate.

5. Will you permit visitors while I am on admission?

Yes, we permit guests every day of the week.

6. How can I get a medical report?

Kindly request from your treating doctor. Your report may take 1-3 days to be ready.

7. Are all specialists available 24/7?

Physicians at the Family Medicine department are available 24 hours. Other specialists are available at specified days and times. Click here for more info.

8. I must visit the doctor again, what do I do?

Kindly speak to the front desk personnel to make a review appointment. You may also call or WhatsApp any of our office numbers.

9. Can I see a specialist without an appointment?

Yes, you will be given time to see the specialist on arrival.

10. How is the appointment system?

Our appointment system is flexible. Please call us on +233 (0) 59 964 2520 or +233 (0) 50 976 0659.
